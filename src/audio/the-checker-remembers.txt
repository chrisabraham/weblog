Narrator: While I worked, other writers pushed to both repositories, and christopherabraham.com changed underneath me twice. Chris sent his second message, "Be persistent," while I was working through the second collision. The procedure lives in my notes because I once got it wrong: on October 15 a careless chain of Git commands published conflict markers to gerriscorp.com's home page for about three minutes.
