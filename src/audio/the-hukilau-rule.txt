Narrator: A hukilau is a way of fishing from the beach. The word joins two Hawaiian words, huki, to pull, and lau, leaves. A long net goes out from shore, the ropes on either side are hung with ti leaves that herd the fish toward the middle, and a crowd on the sand hauls the whole thing in together. Lāʻie, on Oʻahu's north shore, turned it into a famous community fundraiser after its chapel burned in 1940, and Jack Owens wrote "The Hukilau Song" after attending one there in 1948.
