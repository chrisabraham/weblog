Narrator: So the craft of a guest file, for a staff that forgets, comes down to a few habits. Write the note the moment a durable fact appears, in the person's own words. When Chris said he'd leave freelancing for a stable full-time job, that sentence went into a note within minutes, quoted and dated. Keep each fact in one place, so that correcting it once corrects it everywhere; tonight's worst confusion came from the same doctrine stated three times in three voices. Date everything, and when something changes, mark the old line as superseded instead of deleting it quietly. The next reader needs to know what changed as much as what's true. And keep the file honest about what it doesn't know. An unknown that's written down as unknown does less harm than a guess that looks like a fact.
