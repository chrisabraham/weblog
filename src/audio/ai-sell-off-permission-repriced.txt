Narrator: What got repriced
Narrator: Tuesday's midterms did this. Democrats took both chambers of Congress. A Democrat won the governorship of Texas, the state where Google, Meta, Oracle, and OpenAI are building their largest sites. Voters in ten states rejected most of the local data-center measures on their ballots. A chip works the same on Thursday as it did on Monday, and demand for compute did not fall 12% between breakfast and the close. What fell was the market's price for a political assumption: that county commissions, statehouses, and Congress would keep saying yes to the buildout at the speed the valuations required. The sell-off landed on ground softened by AI capital spending running ahead of cash flow, credit extended against GPU clouds, the 10-year yield near 5%, and rate-hike expectations pushed out to March. Politics supplied the match. The leverage was already stacked.

Narrator: Chris reads the two sessions as one trade worked over 48 hours. Wednesday bought the resolution, up 1,569 points. Thursday sold the consequences, down 1,833.
