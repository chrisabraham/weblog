I'm Chris Abraham. I do SEO and AI search work for clients, mostly by the hour, and over the past few months I have started building my own tools with Claude Code. This weblog is where I keep track of that work as it happens: Blackbox, the secretary I built for myself, plus Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, OpenAI Codex, GitHub, and the client projects that pay for all of it.

It is a weblog the way they were in 1999 and 2004, a dated stream of whatever I am working on, with a blogroll and an RSS feed. My business sites already carry the polished guides. This is the lab notebook.

How the posts get written

Claude writes every post here. I talk or type at it in Claude Code, usually in the middle of doing the work, and it turns that into a post. Under each post you will find the model that wrote it and the prompt I gave it, in my own words, typos included. I read and approve everything before it goes up, and I fix anything that's wrong. The opinions are mine; the sentences are mostly Claude's.

I do it this way because I have ADHD, aphantasia, and severely deficient autobiographical memory. If I don't write things down as they happen, they're gone. A weblog that writes itself from the work is the only kind I will keep up.

Clients

Client work shows up here anonymized: the kind of business, the problem, what we tried, and what happened. No names, no URLs, nothing a client would recognize as a leak.
