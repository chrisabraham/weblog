AI Overviews are the generated answers Google places at the top of many search results, above the organic listings. AI Mode is Google's conversational search surface, where a searcher asks follow-up questions and receives a generated answer with source links inline. Both surfaces answer from the live index: Google retrieves pages, composes an answer, and attaches citations to the claims the answer makes. The weblog's AI-search reporting returns to these two surfaces constantly, because they sit between a searcher and every site the searcher might have visited.

How the answer is assembled

An AI Overview begins with retrieval. Google selects pages from its index, reads them at answer time, and writes a summary aimed at the question asked. Links appear inside the answer, attached to sentences, and Google said on October 15, 2026 that it had made substantial increases to inline links in AI Mode and AI Overviews, showing those links higher up in responses more frequently. Google has also tested a dedicated section at the top of some responses for the one or two official places where a task gets completed or exact information lives. Placement inside the answer carries the value: a link attached to the sentence a customer is reading arrives at the moment the claim lands. The post the citation is the new ranking works through that October announcement in full.

How much of search they cover

Coverage trackers measure different samples and land in the same range. Semrush tracked a fixed panel of commercial keywords through August 2026 and found AI Overviews on 88% of them, nearly double the share a year earlier. SE Ranking found an AI Overview on 82.5% of 1,000 US brand keywords checked in October 2026. A randomized field experiment published in 2026 attached a causal figure to the click loss publishers had reported anecdotally: 39.8% fewer clicks on searches carrying an AI Overview. The weblog's post AI Overviews took the click, and now the loss is measured lays out the trackers, the experiment, and the limits of each instrument side by side.

What changes for a site owner

The answer surface rewards pages that state facts plainly. A page that names who the business is, what it does, where it operates, and what a service involves gives the retrieval step clean material to cite. Consistent entity information across a site, its profiles, and its listings helps the machine reconcile sources that describe the same business. The measurement habit changes with the surface: reporting moves from rank positions toward cited-or-absent results on a fixed panel of questions that matter commercially.
