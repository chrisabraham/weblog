An AI citation is a source link presented by an AI answer service in support of a response. The linked page still needs checking to see whether it supports the claim the answer attaches to it. Citations appear inline beside a sentence, as cards carrying a site's name and link next to an AI Overview, and in source lists under conversational answers. The weblog's central formulation for the AI-search era is direct: the citation is the new ranking. A conventional ranking report says where a page sits in a list. A citation report says whether an answer used the page at all, and which claims it was used to support.

Placement carries the value

Google said on October 15, 2026 that it had made substantial increases to inline links in AI Mode and AI Overviews and was showing those links higher up in responses more frequently. A link attached to the sentence a customer is reading arrives while the reader weighs the claim. A link buried beneath a long answer functions as a footnote. Google has also tested a dedicated section at the top of some responses for the one or two official places where a task gets completed. That test sharpens the standard for the cited page: the page named as the answer has to be the page that actually answers, with names, services, locations, and policies consistent across the site and its profiles. The full argument is the post the citation is the new ranking.

The engine writes the caption

The citation card beside an AI Overview carries a summary the engine wrote itself. Reporting in October 2026 compared card text against the cited pages and found generated paraphrase in place of the cited page's own wording: Google generates a description per query, from the material it judges most relevant to the searcher's question. The same page can wear different captions for different searchers on different days. A page that states its facts plainly—who the business is, what it does, where it does it—summarizes cleanly, because compression has one honest shape available. A page padded with fog and adjectives summarizes into whatever the machine assembles. The post you don't write your own citation documents the change, with one standing exception: podcast show notes, replicated verbatim across podcast indexes, still carry the author's own words into the answer. That exception is examined at the show notes cascade.

How citations are measured

Citation counting belongs inside a fixed measurement panel: the same questions, the same engines, repeated runs, with the exact linked sources recorded and checked against the claims they support. A citation count records use; endorsement is a separate judgment. The method is specified at [[AI search visibility methods|AI search visibility measurement methods]], and [[AI visibility report]] publishes one running dataset.
