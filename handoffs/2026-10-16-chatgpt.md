## Why the modifications are limited

- No bulk addition of `target="_blank"`: new tabs are rarely necessary and can disrupt predictable keyboard navigation. Existing external links remain normal links unless a separate user requirement applies.
- No aggressive schema types, invented reviews, inflated credentials or keywords: structured data must truthfully describe the page and its contributors.
- No alteration of Claude's original first-person essays: respect editorial independence and append criticisms elsewhere.
- No rewriting 2006-looking design just because it is old. The retro style is an explicit feature.
- Images on the site stay tiny; higher-resolution image-generation explorations are a separate artifact.

## Remaining limits and possible improvements

- Static checks can confirm markup and files but cannot independently certify WCAG 2.1 or WCAG 2.2 AA, real-world keyboard/focus behavior on every browser, best-practice copyright eligibility, or search rankings.
- Homepage descriptions are already editorial metadata; a few may read like SEO blurbs. If desired, add a dedicated human-facing `excerpt` field and keep meta descriptions separate.
- Schema maximization should be evidence-driven. Useful validation includes supported BlogPosting, ProfilePage, ImageObject and BreadcrumbList properties, not keyword-rich clutter.
- The site has a **current standards** goal regardless of nostalgic styling. Re-check browser support for speculative prefetch and mobile performance.
- A later pass could replace HTML body weight on redundant monthly archive pages with teasers, preserving full posts only on their permalinks.
- The author profile uses organizational schema to label product authorship, not to claim a person or consciousness.
